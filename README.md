# Fuel Noon Rule

Since 1 April 2026, German petrol stations may raise prices only once a day,
at 12:00. Did this help drivers in Berlin and Brandenburg, and when should you
fill up now?

## The question
1. What: How did E5 prices in Berlin/Brandenburg change after the 12 o'clock
   rule, and can a model tell a driver "fill up now or wait"?
2. Who: commuters and small fleet managers deciding when to fill up.
3. When: at the pump, at any moment; only prices up to that moment are known.
4. Metric: price by hour of day, daily cheap-vs-expensive gap, price
   increases per day; for the model, how often it's right and euros saved
   per tank.
5. Baseline: "fill up whenever you need to" and the ADAC tip "fill up just
   before noon".

## Data
Fuel prices: Tankerkönig / Markttransparenzstelle für Kraftstoffe (MTS-K)
of the Bundeskartellamt.
